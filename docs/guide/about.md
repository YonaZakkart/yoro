# About

**Yoro** is a little robot that lives inside a web page.

The first time you visit there's almost nothing: a dark screen, a shy robot and a couple of clumsy sentences. Little by little Yoro discovers he can do things. First he makes a very simple game, then he improves it, then he invents another one. Over time he remembers your name, how many times you've come back and which games you like.

The idea is simple: **Yoro doesn't start out impressive, he has to earn it.** The fun is watching him learn.

## A learning project

Yoro is also a learning exercise. Everything is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, written step by step in small branches, with no frameworks and no build tools. It's published with GitHub Pages.

## Where it is now

- 9 events and 3 games, each one growing in versions (see [Yoro games](games.md)).
- Spanish and English, detected from your browser.
- Yoro was redrawn in SVG (v0.54) with arm gestures, seven facial expressions and moods: he sleeps, gets happy, annoyed, dizzy or angry depending on what you do.

## What's next

- More games and improvements to the current ones.
