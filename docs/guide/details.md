# Details

- **Languages**: Spanish and English. Detected from your browser the first time; change it anytime in Options.
- **Music** (optional, off by default): a calm theme for dialogues and menus, and another one when a game starts. Your choice is remembered.
- **Options panel**: language, change your name, reset stats or reset the whole game.
- **Stats panel**: visits, days visited, games played, best results and per-mode details for each game.
- **Tab title**: if you leave the tab, the title changes to "Are you leaving...?".
- **Pausing**: dialogues pause while the Options or Stats panel is open.
- **Installable**: it can be installed as an app thanks to `manifest.json` and its icons.
- **Accessibility**: respects "reduce motion" (Yoro moves much less).
