# Yoro games

Yoro makes his games one by one, and each game grows in versions as he learns. From the menu you always play the latest version.

## Guess the Number

Yoro's first game: find the number he's thinking of.

| Version | Range | What's new |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 1 - 10 | Just guess |
| 1.1 | 1 - 20 | "Higher" / "lower" hints |
| 1.2 | 1 - 50 | Two modes |

- **Casual**: hints tell you how close you are.
- **Challenge**: miss 5 times in a row and the number escapes (changes).

## Count With Me

Quick sums and subtractions with Yoro.

| Version | What's new |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | 5 problems |
| 1.1 | Two modes, bigger numbers |

- **Casual**: 10 problems, keep your streak.
- **Infinite**: keeps going until your first mistake (negative results included). How long can you last?

## Hangman

Guess letter by letter or go for the whole word. Accents don't matter: typing `o` also reveals `ó`.

| Version | What's new |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | No lives, just find the word |
| 1.1 | ASCII drawing and lives, Casual and Challenge |
| 1.2 | Expert mode |

- **Casual**: 15 lives, you can try the whole word several times.
- **Challenge**: 12 lives, only one try for the whole word.
- **Expert**: 10 lives, longer words (6+ letters), one try for the whole word. After 3 missed letters you can buy a hint (reveals a letter, costs 2 lives). If you lose, Yoro may offer one second chance.

At the end, a results table shows the word, your attempts and the lives you had left.
