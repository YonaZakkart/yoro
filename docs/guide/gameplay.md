# Gameplay

## Events

Yoro moves forward through **events**. Each time you come back, if he has something new to show you, he tells you about it: a new game, a better version of an old one, or a question (like your name).

Events happen one per visit. If you can't wait, the **Skip time...** button (top right, after an event) reloads the page to jump to the next one.

Once Yoro has shown everything, he becomes a small game shelf: a menu where you can replay the latest version of each game.

## Yoro remembers you

Yoro keeps a simple memory in your browser (`localStorage`): your name, how many times you've visited, how many different days you've come, and how you do in each game. Nothing leaves your device.

## Yoro has feelings

Yoro follows your cursor (or finger) with his eyes and reacts to what happens:

- He waves when an event starts or ends, and when you say goodbye.
- He gets happy while talking and playing, and celebrates when you win.
- You can **tap his head or his core**. A few taps make him happy (and he may share a curiosity about your stats). Too many... not so much.
- Typing nonsense or skipping all his lines will annoy him.
- If nobody pays attention to him, he falls asleep. Waking him up has consequences.
- If you leave him sleeping long enough, he might talk in his sleep.
